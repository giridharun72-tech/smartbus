Solving Real Commuter Challenges

The Public Transit Dilemma & The SmartBus Solution

The Common Problems

  • Passengers stand at stops not knowing if their bus has already passed or is running late.
  • Unannounced cancellations and breakdowns leave commuters stranded.
  • Uncertainty about seat availability leading to overcrowded buses.
  • Lost GPS signal renders ordinary tracking apps completely useless.
  • Passengers who forget their phones have no easy way to prove ticket validity.

The SmartBus Solution

  • Real-time GPS Tracking: Live Leaflet maps showing exact coordinates, speed, and ETA.
  • Chronological Time Search: Searching 11:10 AM shows 11:10, 11:15, 11:30 and later buses.
  • Smart Proximity Alerts: Push notification when your bus is 500m, 1km, or 2km from your stop.
  • BCU Manual Location Fallback: If GPS drops, BCU authorizes driver manual entry so tracking stays alive.
  • Universal Verification: Conductor and BCU can verify tickets via QR code, Ticket #, or Passenger ID.
